Ballon d'Or™ Voting Terms and Conditions

1. Purpose of the Vote

The Ballon d'Or of All Time (B.O.A.T.) public vote is organized by France Football and UEFA to allow football fans worldwide to celebrate iconic multi-time winners of the Ballon d'Or.

2. Participation and Fee

A symbolic administrative contribution of €1 is requested for each vote. This fee supports grassroots football equipment initiatives through the Ballon d'Or Foundation.

3. Voter Reward

Every participant receives an immediate digital, high-resolution commemorative voter certificate and high-resolution vintage photograph of their selected champion.

4. Integrity & Transparency

Votes are validated with unique transaction tokens to prevent fraudulent automated bot casting.

The most prestigious individual award in world football, honouring legendary excellence and greatness since 1956.
