Frequently Asked Questions

Who has won the most Ballon d'Or awards in history?
Lionel Messi holds the all-time record with 8 Ballons d'Or (2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, and 2023). Cristiano Ronaldo is second with 5 titles (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, and 2017).
Can a goalkeeper win the Ballon d'Or?
Yes! Lev Yashin, the legendary Dynamo Moscow and Soviet Union shot-stopper known as the "Black Spider", won the Ballon d'Or in 1963. To honour goalkeepers today, France Football established the dedicated Yashin Trophy in 2019.
How does the jury vote for the Ballon d'Or?
A panel of 100 specialized sports journalists representing the top 100 FIFA ranking nations each select a top 10 list from the 30 finalists. Points are awarded: 15, 12, 10, 8, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 point.
